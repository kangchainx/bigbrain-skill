# Bigbrain motion film

Authored by Claude (the advisor) for the README. Codex scaffolds, saves, renders and verifies it. This is an illustrative script, not a real transcript.

## Reference
zkjays (x.com/zkjays/status/2113186873745322233): a cream plane of infographic tiles in perspective, cards lifting into the foreground, one focus per scene. Borrowed: the physical plane, the depth, soft shadows and sequential focus. Not copied: his copy, his gold palette, his numbered step cards.

## Look
Warm ivory plane (#EFE9DD) and ink (#1C1B18). Cobalt (#2E4BD6) marks Claude; lime (#B5D334) marks Codex and success; brick red (#C2412D) is used once, for the failure. System fonts only: New York or Songti SC for Claude and titles, SF Pro or PingFang SC for Codex, SF Mono for the prompt. Codex cards sit left and Claude cards right. Every card enters from the right and leaves to the left while the camera trucks across the plane.

## Story (24 s, 30 fps, 1920x1080)
- 0.0 Open: the plane rises; Two agents. One task.
- 2.2 You: Use Bigbrain for this chat. Bigbrain turns on.
- 4.8 Codex: the spec is fuzzy, so it wants a plan. Task: stop duplicate orders.
- 8.0 Claude: the plan, one idempotency key per order; done when a double click makes one order.
- 11.8 Codex: still two orders.
- 14.6 Claude: the key is made too late; create it when the form opens.
- 17.8 Codex: fixed, the test passes, its first review is clean; a Claude second review only if you ask.
- 21.8 End: the camera pulls back, the tiles ripple, Bigbrain.

## Truth rules
Opt-in is explicit. Claude plans and diagnoses. Codex implements, tests and does the first review. A Claude second review happens only on request. No savings figures and no metrics. The caption stays on every frame.
